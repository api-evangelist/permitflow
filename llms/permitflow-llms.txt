## About Us


PermitFlow is a comprehensive construction permit management software platform designed to simplify and accelerate the permit process for construction teams, projects and jobs across the United States. By automating and streamlining permit research, preparation, submission, status tracking and management, PermitFlow helps builders, developers, contractors, and other stakeholders eliminate delays, ensure compliance, and keep projects on schedule. PermitFlow offers nationwide coverage, tailored solutions for various industry roles, and expert resources to navigate complex municipal and state permitting requirements.


## What We Do (Features)


- [Permit Preparation & Submission](https://www.permitflow.com/product): Prepare and submit permits in days, in some cases as quickly as within 24 hours, with automated workflows and document management.
- [Permit Tracking & Management](https://www.permitflow.com/product): Track permit status and manage all permits across projects and municipalities in one dashboard.
- [National Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) Coverage](https://www.permitflow.com/): Nationwide support for permitting across all U.S. states and local jurisdictions.
- [Tailored Solutions for Industry Stakeholders](https://www.permitflow.com/solutions/): Dedicated workflows for homebuilders, developers, general contractors, subcontractors, home services contractors, architects, solar/EV professionals and retail operations.
- [Permit Timeline Acceleration](https://www.permitflow.com/topic/expedity): Expedite the permit process with expert guidance and automation.
- [Resource Center & Municipal Guides](https://www.permitflow.com/resources): Access comprehensive guides, FAQs, and learning materials for permitting in every state and major municipality.
- [Integration with Construction Platforms](https://www.permitflow.com/blog/permitflow-procore-integration): Integrate with tools like ServiceTitan, Procore and Autodesk for seamless permit management directly within the tools your team already uses.
- [Fee Calculation & Compliance](https://www.permitflow.com/topic/fees): Tools and guidance to understand and manage permit-related fees and compliance requirements.
- [Permit Research](https://www.permitflow.com/resources): In-depth research tools for local codes, zoning, and permit requirements.
